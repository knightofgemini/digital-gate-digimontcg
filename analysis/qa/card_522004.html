Q1366
When a green level 5 Digimon with a [When Attacking] effect attacks, in what order can this card's [Your Turn] effect and the attacking Digimon's [When Attacking] effect activate?Mar. 13, 2026 Updated
A1366
The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order.
However, if this card's [Your Turn] effect is activated first and that Digimon digivolves, its [When Attacking] effect is considered to be lost and can no longer activate.
Q1367
My green Digimon that isn't level 5 attacks, and another effect digivolves it into a green level 5 Digimon.
Can I then activate this card's [Your Turn] effect?Mar. 13, 2026 Updated
A1367
No, you can't activate it.
This card's [Your Turn] effect triggers when a green level 5 Digimon attacks. It doesn't trigger when other Digimon attack, therefore it can't be activated.

Q1388
What does "end the attack" mean, exactly?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A1388
After this effect activates, the current timing makes a transition to the end of attack timing. For example, if this effect activates during the attack declaration timing, it will make a transition to the end of attack timing. A transition to the counter timing or block timing won't occur, and the attack won't succeed.
