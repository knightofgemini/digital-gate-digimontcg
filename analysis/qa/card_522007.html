You activate this card's 1st effect first.
You declare digivolution of this card, then choose whether or not to activate this card's 1st effect immediately before choosing the card to digivolve.
After that, you choose your Digimon to digivolve and process [EX1-071 Win Rate: 60%!]'s "would digivolve" effect.
Additionally, in cases where you would digivolve from a Tamer, the Tamer is treated as a Digimon while it is digivolving, so [EX1-071 Win Rate: 60%!]'s effect can be still processed as well.
