Q3399
I used this card's [Start of Opponent's Main Phase] to suspend my [EX3-044 Breakdramon] or [EX3-074 Examon] and had my opponent attack.
At such times, how do I process effects such as "when this Digimon is suspended" effects on these Digimon and [When Attacking] effects on the attacking Digimon?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A3399
When this Digimon is suspended effects and the [When Attacking] effect are triggered by a single effect, therefore they are considered to trigger simultaneously.
According to the simultaneous trigger rules, first the turn player's [When Attacking] effects will activate, then the non-turn player's "when this Digimon is suspended" effects will activate.

A3400

With this card's effect, your opponent's actions or effects can't play Digimon cards with 5000 DP or less.
This effect affects the opponent player.

A4670

Q5722
Do I delete the Digimon that was played by this card's <Delay> effect at the end of my opponent's turn?
And if it isn't deleted, is it still prevented from digivolving to level 7?Dec. 25, 2025 Updated
A5722
No, you only delete it at the end of your opponent's first turn after it's played by this effect.
If another effect prevents it from being deleted at the end of your opponent's first turn when it would be deleted, that Digimon isn't deleted at the end of your opponent's following turns.
If it isn't deleted by another effect when it would be deleted by this effect, it's still prevented from digivolving to level 7.
Q5723
What is the processing order for an effect that triggers at the end of the turn and the deletion of a Digimon played by this card's <Delay> effect?Dec. 25, 2025 Updated
A5723
The pending processing for the effect that triggers at the end of the turn and the deletion at the end of the turn are considered to be processing that triggers simultaneously. Therefore, the turn player can choose the processing order.
