A2378

Normally, the turn will end and switch when the memory moves to 1 or more on your opponent's side. But while this effect is activated, the turn won't end unless the memory moves to 3 or more on your opponent's side. Your turn will continue when your opponent's memory is at 1 or 2.
Q2450
If the memory moves to 2 or less on my opponent's side during my opponent's turn, does the turn switch to my turn with this card's [Your Turn] effect?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A2450
No, this effect only applies during your turn. Your opponent's turn continues until the memory moves to 1 or more from your perspective.
Q2451
The memory moved to 3 or more on my opponent's side and the turn ended while this card's [Your Turn] effect was activated. If the memory moves to 1 or 2 on my opponent's side due to an [End of Your Turn] effect, does my turn continue?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A2451
Yes, your turn continues.
Q2452
When the memory moves to 3 or more on my opponent's side while this card's [Your Turn] effect is activated, does <Blitz> allow for attacking?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A2452
No. <Blitz> still requires the memory to be at 1 or more on your opponent's side for attacking.
Q2453
Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to play just 2 of the target cards from my trash when [Eiji Nagasumi] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards and I have 3 or more level 4 or lower cards with the [Dark Animal] or [SoC] trait in my trash?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A2453
No, you can't. This effect is a "You may play" effect, but it doesn't include "up to," therefore you must choose to either play no cards or as many as possible.
Q2454
What happens if I declare a "pass" when I have this Digimon?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A2454
The memory will move to 3 on your opponent's side, and the turn will end if there are no other effects pending activation, etc.

Q2479
What happens when a card affected by "change 1 of your opponent's Digimon into a white Digimon with 3000 DP and an original name of [Sukamon]" also has "(Rule) Name: Also treated as [AA]" or "(Rule) Name: Treated as including [AA]"?Sep. 20, 2024 Updated

The text "(Rule) Name: Treated as including [AA]" remains unchanged, and while its name will be changed to [Sukamon], it can also be referenced as a Digimon with [AA] in its name.
Q2480

The card information gained from the effect remains as-is.
For example, if EX1-005 [Tyrannomon]'s original card information is changed, its original color will become white, and EX1-005 [Tyrannomon]'s effect will also treat it as green, making it a 2-color white and green Digimon.
