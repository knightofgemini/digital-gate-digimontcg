Q6106
If a Tamer is also treated as a Digimon using an "is also treated as a Digimon with X000 DP" effect, and later it gains another "is also treated as a Digimon with X000 DP" effect, what happens?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6106
If an effect has triggered and the card is affected by a "is also treated as a Digimon with X000 DP" effect, the play cost, level, and DP are overwritten by the newer effect.
However, if an effect has already activated and the card is affected by a "is also treated as a Digimon with X000 DP" effect, the already activated effect will be overwritten.
Any other effects such as <Rush> that are later gained will be added.

A6109

Q6916
What does this card's [All Turns] effect do exactly?Nov. 20, 2026 Updated
A6916
It triggers when a Digimon is played or digivolves.
You can choose whether or not to process "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon and unsuspend this Digimon." Then, if it was played or digivolved by effects, you can choose whether or not to process "return 1 of your opponent's suspended Digimon to the bottom of the deck."
If you process "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon and unsuspend this Digimon," you must process as much of it as possible, and you can't choose to process only one half or the other.
Choosing to process either of the two actions will use up the [Once per Turn] limit.
