522113 ST13-01 Sakuttomon
Q764
If I play 2 or more Digimon with the [Legend-Arms] trait at the same time, do I gain 1 memory for each Digimon played with this card's inherited effect?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A764
No, even if you play 2 or more Digimon with the [Legend-Arms] trait at the same time with 1 effect, you can only gain 1 memory with this card's inherited effect.
Q765
If I have 2 Digimon with this card in their digivolution cards, and play 1 Digimon with the [Legend-Arms] trait, can I activate both inherited effects and gain 2 memory?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A765
Yes, in that situation you can gain 2 memory in total.
522113 ST13-02 Zubamon
Q766
Can I choose to not activate this card's [On Play] effect?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A766
Yes, the effect reads "By placing this Digimon under 1 of your other Digimon [...] as its bottom digivolution card," so you may also choose to not place it as the bottom digivolution card. In such cases, it remains in the battle area as a Digimon like normal.
Q767
When I reveal a card with the [Legend-Arms] trait and a play cost of 7 or less from my deck for this card's [On Play] effect, can I choose to not play that card?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A767
Yes, the effect reads "you may play it," so you may also choose to not play it. In such cases, you add it to your hand like any other card.
Q768
I revealed a card with the [Legend-Arms] trait and a play cost of 7 or less from my deck for this card's [On Play] effect] when I had a [BT9-047 Pomumon]. What happens to that card?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A768
It can't be played due to [BT9-047 Pomumon]'s effect, so you add it to your hand like any other card.

522113 ST13-04 Duramon
Q770
How does this card’s inherited effect work, exactly?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A770
At the end of your turn, this effect allows you to DNA digivolve the Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards, along with one of your other Digimon, into a Digimon that has a DNA digivolve requirement from your hand. Note that this happens before it becomes your opponent’s turn.
Q771
With this card's inherited effect, can I DNA digivolve into a Digimon card in my hand that doesn’t have a DNA digivolve requirement?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A771
No. You can’t ignore the digivolution requirements, so you can’t DNA digivolve into a Digimon card that doesn’t have a DNA digivolve requirement.
Q772
Does this card's inherited effect allow me to DNA digivolve into a card other than the Digimon specified by [DNA Digivolution] in the hand?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A772
No. You can’t ignore the digivolution requirements, so you can’t DNA digivolve using Digimon that aren’t specified in [DNA Digivolution].
522113 ST13-05 Durandamon
Q773
If I reveal a Digimon card with the [Legend-Arms] trait and a play cost of 7 or less with this card's [When Attacking] effect, can I choose to not play that card?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A773
Yes, "You may play" means you can choose to play it or not. If you don't play it, return it to the bottom of the deck with the rest of the cards.
Q774
If there's a [BT9-047 Pomumon] and I reveal a Digimon card with the [Legend-Arms] trait and a play cost of 7 or less with this card's [When Attacking] effect, what happens to that card?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A774
It can't be played due to [BT9-Pomumon]'s effect, so return it to the bottom of the deck with the rest of the cards.

522113 ST13-06 RagnaLoardmon
Q775
When I DNA digivolve into this card, is it possible to attack with the <Blitz> in the [When Digivolving] effect?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A775
Yes, [When Digivolving] <Blitz> can activate so long as the opponent somehow has 1 or more memory at the time.
Q776
When I DNA digivolve into this card and it has 8 digivolution cards, do I delete 2 of my opponent's Digimon with play costs of 20 or less and trash the top 2 cards of their security stack?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A776
Yes, that's correct.
Q777
When I DNA digivolve into this card, my opponent has 1 or more memory.
If there is an inherited [When Attacking] effect when I attack with the <Blitz> in the [When Digivolving] effect, do I activate the [When Attacking] effect before this card's "for every 4 cards in this Digimon's digivolution cards, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost of 20 or less and trash the top card of your opponent's security stack" effect?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A777
No, this card's <Blitz> and "for every 4 cards in this Digimon's digivolution cards, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost of 20 or less and trash the top card of your opponent's security stack" are a single effect, therefore if DNA digivolving, the latter part of the effect will continue to activate after the attack has been declared and won't be interrupted by another effect.
After the whole effect has finished activating, the [When Attacking] effect will activate.
522113 ST13-08 Chikurimon
Q778
What does this card's "Players can't reduce play costs" effect do, exactly?Sep. 5, 2025 Updated
A778
It negates effects that read, “reduce the play cost by X.” When you or your opponent activates an effect like that, it won't reduce the play cost that needs to be paid. The full play cost of the card needs to be paid.
Q779
Can I omit paying the play cost by a "play this card without paying the cost" effect when I have this Digimon?Sep. 5, 2025 Updated
A779
Yes, "play this card without paying the cost" effects do not reduce the play cost, so this card does not interfere with them.
Q780
Can the play cost of a Digimon with the [Royal Knight] trait be reduced due to the [Your Turn] effect of my opponent's [BT13-007 King Drasil_7D6] if this Digimon is in play?Sep. 5, 2025 Updated
A780
No, they can't be reduced. This card's effect does not target Digimon, so even if your opponent's [BT13-007 King Drasil_7D6] is in the breeding area, its effect to reduce play costs can't be activated.
Q781
When I have this Digimon, can my opponent no longer use [BT14-046 Togemon]'s [Your Turn] effect or inherited effect to reduce the play cost of green Tamer cards?Sep. 5, 2025 Updated
A781
That's correct. Such effects can no longer reduce play costs.
This card's effect also prevents play cost-reducing effects from activating when Tamer cards are played.
