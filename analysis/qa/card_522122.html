Q5407
If the "when you use an Option card" effect triggers, what is the timing when it can activate?Nov. 21, 2025 Updated
A5407
It can be activated after activating the used Option card's [Main] effect.
Q5408
Does the "when you use an Option card" effect trigger if an Option card's effect activates by a method other than using the card?Nov. 21, 2025 Updated
A5408
No, it doesn't trigger.
If an Option card's effect activates without using it, such as activation by a [Security] effect or <Delay>, the "when you use an Option card" effect doesn't trigger.

522122 ST22-04 Taomon
Q5410
What does "[When Digivolving] effects don't activate" mean, exactly?Dec. 25, 2025 Updated
A5410
This effect prevents [When Digivolving] effects on cards from activating.
[When Digivolving] effects can no longer activate by triggering or when effects such as "activate that card's [When Digivolving]" effect" activate.
Q5411
I used a card affected by "can't activate [When Digivolving] effects" to attack.
Can I activate that card's [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect at such times?Dec. 25, 2025 Updated
A5411
Yes, you can.
The effect can activate if it activates upon the [When Attacking] timing.
Q5412
If a card is affected by "can't activate [When Digivolving] effects," can other effects activate its [When Digivolving] effect?Dec. 25, 2025 Updated
A5412
No, it can't be activated.
Q5413
If a card is affected by "can't activate [When Digivolving] effects," can I process just the "by" condition in its [When Digivolving] effect?Nov. 21, 2025 Updated
A5413
No, you can't.
Q5414
A card that was given "can't activate [When Digivolving] effects" digivolved into a card that has a [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] effect.
Does that count as an instance of that effect's [X Per Turn] even if that effect can't be activated upon the timing when that card digivolved?Dec. 25, 2025 Updated
A5414
No, it doesn't count.
The effect can't activate upon the [When Digivolving] timing, therefore it doesn't count as an instance of that effect's [X Per Turn].
Q5415
If I use this card's [When Attacking] effect to use an Option card from under a Tamer, what happens to that Option card?Dec. 25, 2025 Updated
A5415
As with the standard rules, a used Option card is temporarily treated as not being in any area.
After using it and activating the Option card's [Main] effect, the card is trashed unless it's to be placed in an area.
Q5416
During an attack by this card, I used the inherited effect on ST22-01 [Viximon] in this card's digivolution cards to digivolve it. Does this card's [End of Attack] inherited effect trigger at the end of the attack?Jun. 19, 2026 Updated
A5416
Yes, it triggers.

A5420

Q5424
In what order do a [Security] effect, "when [...] performs a security check" effect, and "when a card is removed from [...] security stack" effect activate when they trigger simultaneously upon a security check?Nov. 21, 2025 Updated
A5424
[Security] effects take precedence for activation.
Upon a security check, a [Security] effect will immediately activate without pending activation.
For other triggered effects, the turn player activates their effects first.
Q5425
I tried to use this card's [All Turns] effect to place an opponent's Digimon as the bottom security card, but another effect prevented that Digimon from leaving.
At such times, does this effect trash an opponent's security card?Nov. 21, 2025 Updated
A5425
No, it isn't trashed.
Q5426
I tried to use this card's [All Turns] effect to place an opponent's Digimon as the bottom security card, but another effect prevented that Digimon from leaving.
At such times, does it count as a use of this effect's [Once Per Turn]?Nov. 21, 2025 Updated
A5426
Yes, it counts. A use of [X Per Turn] occurs as soon as you choose to activate the effect.
In this case, a use of [Once Per Turn] occurs as soon as you choose to place an opponent's Digimon as the bottom security card.

522122 ST22-10 Amethyst Mandala
Q5435
If this card's 1st effect and a "when a card is removed from [...] security stack" effect trigger simultaneously when this card is trashed from the security stack, in what order do they activate?Nov. 21, 2025 Updated
A5435
The turn player activates their effect first.
If both effects are the turn player's, they can activate them in any order.
Q5436
What is a {Security} effect?Nov. 21, 2025 Updated
A5436
A {Security} effect can be triggered/activated while its card is face up in the security stack.
Such effects can't be triggered or activated in areas other than the face-up security cards.
Q5437
I have multiple copies of this card face-up in my security stack.
When my Digimon with [Renamon], [Kyubimon], [Taomon], or [Sakuyamon] in its name would leave the battle area other than by battle, I activate the {Security} [All Turns] effect on the 1st copy of this card, and that Digimon is prevented from leaving. Can I then activate the {Security} [All Turns] effect on the 2nd copy of this card?Nov. 21, 2025 Updated
A5437
Yes, you can.
The {Security} [All Turns] effects on the 2 copies of this card trigger simultaneously when your Digimon with [Renamon], [Kyubimon], [Taomon], or [Sakuyamon] in its name would leave the battle area other than by battle. After activating the effect on the 1st copy, the Digimon is prevented from leaving, then you can activate the {Security} [All Turns] effect on the 2nd copy of this card.
After that, the 1st effect on the 2 copies of this card will trigger simultaneously, and you can activate them one at a time for a total of 2 activations.
Q5438
I have this card placed face-up in my security stack.
If this card's {Security} [All Turns] effect activates when multiples of my Digimon with [Renamon], [Kyubimon], [Taomon], or [Sakuyamon] in their names would leave the battle area other than by battle, then this card is trashed from the security stack, what happens?Nov. 21, 2025 Updated
A5438
You choose 1 of your Digimon with [Renamon], [Kyubimon], [Taomon], or [Sakuyamon] in its name, and that Digimon is prevented from leaving the battle area.
If you have another face-up copy of this card in your security stack, the {Security} [All Turns] effects on those copies will also activate, and you can choose 1 more Digimon that will be prevented from leaving.

522122 ST22-14 Barbamon
Q5447
My opponent has 5 cards in their hand and 5 cards in their trash.
Can I activate this card's 1st effect and play it with the play cost reduced by 5?Nov. 21, 2025 Updated
A5447
No, you can't.
This card's 1st effect activates when your opponent has 10 or more cards in their hand or 10 or more cards in their trash, then the play cost will be reduced by 5.
It doesn't activate when the total number of cards in their hand and trash is 10.
Q5448
My opponent has 10 cards in their hand and 10 cards in their trash.
If I activate this card's 1st effect, what is the reduction to the play cost?Nov. 21, 2025 Updated
A5448
The play cost is reduced by 5.
Even if they have 10 cards in their hand and 10 cards in their trash, the reduction to the play cost will be 5.
