Basic Rules Building a Deck
Q1
Can I include 4 copies each of [ST1-03 Agumon] and [BT1-010 Agumon] in my deck?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A1
Yes. Cards are identified by their card number. As long as their card numbers are different, you can include 4 copies of two cards with the same name in your deck.
Q2
Can I include Digi-Egg cards in my deck?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A2
No, you can't.
Q3
Can I include cards other than Digi-Egg cards in my Digi-Egg deck?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A3
No, you can't.
Q4
Can I play without a Digi-Egg deck?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A4
Yes. Digi-Egg decks can include between 0-5 cards, and players are free to not use a Digi-Egg deck at all.
Q5
Can I include five copies of the same card in my Digi-Egg deck?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A5
No. Like your regular deck, you can only include up to 4 copies of a card with the same card number in your Digi-Egg deck.
Q6
My deck has 45 cards, and my Digi-Egg deck has 5, adding up to a total of 50. Is my deck legal?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A6
No. Your deck must contain 50 cards on its own, with or without cards from your Digi-Egg deck.
Basic Rules Game Preparation
Q7
If I don't like my starting hand, can I shuffle the cards back into my deck and redraw?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A7
Yes. You can re-draw your hand while preparing a game, but you can only do this once per game.
When re-drawing, return your entire hand to your deck, shuffle it, then draw 5 new cards. You must re-draw your entire hand, not just part of it.
The first player first declares whether or not they will re-draw.
Q8
If I win rock paper scissors (or a coin flip, etc.) before the game begins, can I decide which player goes first?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A8
No, the player who wins rock paper scissors automatically goes first.
Q9
Do we have to determine who goes first before drawing our starting hands?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A9
Yes. Determine who goes first, then draw your starting hand.
Q10
When placing cards in my security stack, can I choose any cards I like from my deck?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A10
No. Place cards from the top of your deck in your security stack, one at a time. If done properly, the top card of your deck should be the bottom card of your security stack.
Q11
Do I have to prepare token cards?Sep. 13, 2024 Updated
A11
If your deck or Digi-Egg deck includes a card with an effect that uses tokens, you must prepare token cards.
If your deck or Digi-Egg deck doesn't include such a card, you don't have to prepare token cards.
Basic Rules Unsuspend Phase
Q12
During this phase, can I unsuspend Tamers that have been suspended?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A12
Yes. Suspended Tamers are unsuspended during this phase.
Q13
Can I choose not to unsuspend Digimon and/or Tamers during this phase?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A13
No, suspended cards must be unsuspended during this phase.
Q14
During this phase, do I also unsuspend my opponent's Digimon and Tamers?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A14
No. Only the player whose turn it is gets to unsuspend their Digimon and Tamers.
Q15
Do [Start of Your Turn] effects trigger during the unsuspend phase of my turn?Jun. 13, 2025 Updated
A15
Yes, they trigger during the unsuspend phase of your turn.
The effects can trigger and activate before the unsuspending actions in the unsuspend phase.
Basic Rules Draw Phase
Q16
Can I choose not to draw cards during this phase?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A16
No, you can't. If there are 1 or more cards in your deck, you must draw from it. If you have to draw but there are no cards left in your deck, you lose the game.
Q17
Is there a maximum hand size?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A17
No, you can have as many cards in your hand as you like.
Basic Rules Breeding Phase
Q18
Can I choose not to hatch a Digi-Egg or move a level 3+ Digimon from the breeding area to the battle area?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A18
Yes, both hatching a new Digi-Egg and moving Digimon from the breeding area to the battle area are optional.
Q19
Can I hatch a new Digi-Egg when a Digimon is already in my breeding area?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A19
No, your breeding area must be empty.
Q20
Can I trash the Digimon in my breeding area to hatch a new Digi-Egg card?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A20
No, you can't. Digimon and Tamers can't be trashed or returned to your hand or deck unless the game rules require it.
Q21
Do I lose the game when my Digi-Egg deck runs out of cards?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A21
No. You won't be able to hatch new Digi-Egg cards, but you won't lose the game.
Q22
Do I digivolve Digimon in my breeding area during my breeding phase?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A22
No, digivolving Digimon in your breeding area takes place during your main phase.
Q23
I move a Digimon from my breeding area to my battle area. Does the Digimon's [On Play] effect activate?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A23
No, it doesn't. Moving a Digimon to the battle area doesn't count as playing it.
Basic Rules Main Phase
Q24
Can I digivolve a Digimon that just entered play?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A24
Yes, you can.
Q25
I digivolve a suspended Digimon. Does this unsuspend it?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A25
No. If you digivolve a suspended Digimon, it remains suspended.
Q26
If I digivolve into a Digimon that has an [On Play] effect, does it activate?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A26
No. [On Play] effects don't activate with digivolution.
Q27
I digivolve a Digimon in my breeding area. Does its [When Digivolving] effect activate?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A27
No, you can't activate effects on Digimon in your breeding area.
Q28
I have a Digimon whose power is being modified by an effect. What happens if I digivolve it?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A28
It depends on the effect. If the effect lacks specific conditions, or if the effect asks you to choose a Digimon that meets conditions at the time of activation, the effect persists following digivolution.
If the effect applies to all Digimon that meet specific conditions, and the Digimon no longer meets those conditions following digivolution, the effect ends.
Q29
If a card has a play/use cost of 11 or more, can I use it if your memory gauge is at zero?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A29
No. From zero, you will only be able to move your memory counter to 10 on your opponent's side, but you need to move it to 11.
Q30
Can I use effects from Digimon, Tamers, and Options on Digimon in my breeding area?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A30
No, you can't.
Digimon in breeding areas aren't affected by card effects, and can't activate effects themselves.
Q31
I play a Digimon with an [On Play] effect or digivolve a Digimon with an [When Digivolving] effect, and paying the play/digivolve cost results in my memory counter moving to 1 on my opponent's side. Does my Digimon's [On Play] or [When Digivolving] effect activate?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A31
Yes, it does. After resolving the effect on the card you paid for, or resolving effects resulting from that card, it becomes your opponent's turn.
Q32
Do effects with conditions like "If you have a Digimon with <X>" count Digimon in my breeding area?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A32
No, Digimon in your breeding area don't count.
Q33
Multiple card effects have activated at the same time. What order should I activate them in?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A33
When multiple effects trigger for one player, the player of those cards chooses the order they activate in.
When choosing the order, the next effect to activate is chosen each time one effect activates, but if activating an effect causes a new effect to trigger, the new effect takes priority for activation.
Q34
Multiple card effects have activated at the same time for both me and my opponent. What order should we activate them in?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A34
When both players activate effects at the same time, the turn player starts by activating their card effects in whatever order they like.
Then, the non-turn player activates their card effects in whatever order they like.
When choosing the order, the next effect to activate is chosen each time one effect activates, but if activating an effect causes a new effect to trigger, the new effect takes priority for activation, regardless of the player of that effect.
Q35
When a Digimon or Tamer is deleted, returned to hand, or otherwise leaves play, do their effects applying to other cards end?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A35
It depends on the effect. If the effect timing is [Your Turn], [Opponent's Turn], or [All Turns], the effect ends once the Digimon or Tamer that activated the effect leaves play.
If the effect lasts for a length of time specified by the card text ("for the turn," "until the end of your opponent's next turn," etc.), the effect persists even if the card that activated the effect leaves play.
Q36
I play an Option card, and paying the play/digivolve cost results in my memory counter moving to 1 on my opponent's side. Does the Option card's effect activate?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A36
Yes, it does. After resolving the effect on the card you paid for, or resolving effects resulting from that card, it becomes your opponent's turn.
Q37
Is it possible for a Digimon's DP to be negative?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A37
No, zero is the minimum. A Digimon with a DP of zero is deleted.
Q38
I have an effect with [Once Per Turn] that I can choose whether or not to activate. If I choose not to activate the effect, can I activate it later in the same turn, or does the [Once Per Turn] restriction prevent me from doing so?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A38
If you chose not to use it, the [Once Per Turn] restriction doesn't apply. You can choose to use it later in the turn.
Q39
When multiple card effects activate, do I decide the order of their resolution at the start, or can I choose which effect to resolve individually as I go?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A39
Choose the first effect to activate. When it finishes, choose one of the remaining effects to activate, repeating this process until all effects have been activated. If activating an effect causes a new effect to trigger, the new effect is activated first.
Q40
If a card effect gives me more than 10 memory, what happens?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A40
You can't have more than 10 memory. Any memory exceeding 10 is lost.
Q41
If an effect requires me to return multiple cards to my deck, do I need to tell my opponent the order I return the cards in?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A41
Yes. The order you return cards in is public information.
Q42
Are [When Digivolving] effects activated before or after I draw my bonus card for digivolving?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A42
Activate the effect after you draw the card for digivolving.
Q43
I have a Digimon with a keyword effect that activates at a specific time -- for example, [On Play]. Can I choose it with an effect that specifies Digimon with that keyword, even outside its activation timing?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A43
Yes, you can. The Digimon has the keyword effect, even if it isn't currently being activated.
Q44
I have a Digimon that gains a keyword effect when certain conditions are met. For example: "While you have 3 or fewer security cards, this Digimon gains <Blocker>." If I have an effect that specifies Digimon with <Blocker>, does it count this Digimon even when I have 4 or more security cards?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A44
No, it doesn't. Digimon that gain keywords under certain conditions or from an effect are only considered to have that keyword when in play in the battle area, and when the condition is being met. They are not considered to have the keyword at other times.
Q45
I have a Digimon with multiple effects that last "for the turn," or "until the end of your opponent's next turn." Do these effects end in a certain order at the end of the turn?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A45
There's no order. The effects end simultaneously at the end of the turn. If you had a Digimon with two effects, one that added DP and one that subtracted it, even if losing the positive DP bonus would cause the Digimon to reach 0 DP and leave play, the effects would end simultaneously, returning your Digimon to its original DP.
Q46
Do effects with [On Play], [When Digivolving], [On Deletion], [When Attacking] have to resolve at the timing specified? For example, if I have an effect with [On Deletion], am I required to activate it when the Digimon is deleted?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A46
Yes, whenever possible. However, if the effect has "can" in its text, you can choose whether or not to activate it.
Effects that read "you may" (or "do <X> to do <Y>") are optional, and can be chosen not to be activated.
Q47
My opponent only has one Digimon in play. Can I use an effect that targets two of my opponent's Digimon? (For example: "Suspend 2 of your opponent's Digimon."Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A47
Yes, the effect can still be activated even if there are less Digimon in play than the effect specifies.
Q48
My opponent has two Digimon in play. Can I use an effect that targets two of my opponent's Digimon and only choose one of them? (For example: "Suspend 2 of your opponent's Digimon."Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A48
It depends on the effect.
・If the effect reads, "2 of your opponent's Digimon," it must affect two Digimon.
・If the effect reads, "up to 2 of your opponent's Digimon," you can choose any number of Digimon to use the effect on, up to 2.
Q49
For effects that refer to a "card with [X] in its name," if a card name includes [X] as part of its name, does it count even if it's not a perfect match?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A49
Yes. Effects that check for [X] in card names don't need the names to be a perfect match.
For example, an effect that reads, "1 Digimon card with [Agumon] in its name" applies to "Agumon Expert," "ToyAgumon," and "BushiAgumon" alike.
Q50
If the Option card revealed upon a security check has a [Security] effect and it activates, does it count as "using an Option card"?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A50
No. Even if an Option card revealed upon a security check has a [Security] effect and it activates, it doesn't count as using an Option card.
"Using an Option card" refers to when you use an Option card from your hand by paying its cost, or when you activate an effect on a card with text such as "use an Option card" or "you may use an Option card."
Q51
During a match, can I check the number of cards in my opponent's deck/Digi-egg deck/trash/hand or check the front side of cards in their trash?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A51
Yes. That information is considered public, so you can ask your opponent to allow you to check it.
Q52
Can I choose to not activate a card's effect?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A52
It depends on the effect.
If an effect reads "by X, Y" or similar, you can choose to not activate the effect by not performing "X."
If an effect reads "you may" or similar, you can choose to not activate that effect.
If an effect uses imperative verbs such as "play" or "use," you must activate the effect whenever possible.
Q53
Are effects that last for the turn such as [Your Turn] and "for the turn" effects lost once effects that activate at the end of the turn are processed?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A53
No. Effects that last for the turn will continue activating until the turn switches, therefore the turn switch will not occur during processing of effects such as effects that activate at the end of the turn, and the effects that last for the turn will still be activated.
Q54
When the turn ends by the memory moving to 1 or more on my opponent's side, what happens if an effect activates and returns the memory to 0 or more on my side?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A54
If the memory returns to 0 or more after processing all effects that activate/apply at the end of the turn, the turn will continue instead of ending.
Q55
When the turn ends by the memory moving to 1 or more on my opponent's side, if an effect activates and returns the memory to 0 or more on my side and my turn continues, what happens to effects activates that last for the turn, such as [Your Turn] and "for the turn" effects that have already activated?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A55
Effects that last for the turn will continue activating until the turn switches, therefore the effects will still be activated if the turn continues. The effects won't pause.
Q56
At the end of the turn, an effect activates and returns the memory to 0 or more on my side, and my turn continues. If the memory again moves to 1 or more on my opponent's side, do effects that activate at the end of the turn activate again?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A56
Yes, they activate.
Q57
I paid the play cost in order to play a Digimon card with an [On Play] effect, and this caused the memory to move to 1 or more on my opponent's side. Do effects that activate at the end of the turn and this Digimon's [On Play] effect trigger simultaneously?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A57
No, they don't trigger simultaneously. The [On Play] effect activates first.
Even if the memory moves to 1 or more on the opponent's side due to paying a cost, etc., the end of the turn doesn't come immediately. If the memory moves to 1 or more on the opponent's side after processing has completed for all effects that have activated or triggered, then the end of the turn will come.
Q58
If I don't have any Digimon that meet the digivolution requirements for a Digimon card in my hand, can I deliberately play it so its digivolution will fail and I trash it?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A58
No, you can't.
If a card can't be digivolved, it can't be revealed from the hand.
Q59
Can I cancel a digivolution after playing the Digimon to digivolve into from my hand?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A59
No, you can't.
Once you reveal the Digimon to digivolve into from your hand, you can't cancel the digivolution.
Q60
When do I declare the digivolution requirements that I use to digivolve a Digimon?Apr. 19, 2024 Updated
A60
You declare the digivolution requirement used for a digivolution when you reveal the card to digivolve into to your opponent.
After choosing one of your Digimon that meets the declared digivolution requirements, the Digimon digivolves.
Q61
My opponent activated a "reduce the DP of all your opponent's Digimon" effect and my Digimon's DP became 0 at the same time it was played. In such cases, does that Digimon's [On Play] effect activate before it's deleted?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A61
No, it doesn’t activate before being deleted.
If DP becomes 0 at the same time as a card being played, the [On Play] effect is triggered, but it can't activate because the card is deleted before it activates.
Q62
My opponent activated a "reduce the DP of all your opponent's Digimon" effect and my Digimon's DP became 0 at the same time it was played. In such cases, do "when your Digimon is played" effects on my Digimon and Tamers activate?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A62
Yes, they activate.
Q63
When a Digimon becomes a digivolution card by digivolution or an effect, do the effects on that Digimon no longer activate?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A63
Yes. Effects on Digimon cards no longer activate after they become digivolution cards. Their inherited effects can be activated instead.
Q64
When a Digimon becomes a digivolution card by digivolution or an effect, are the effects on that Digimon negated?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A64
It depends on the effect. If the effect timing is [Your Turn], [Opponent's Turn], or [All Turns], the effect ends once the Digimon card that activated the effect becomes a digivolution card.
If the effect lasts for a length of time specified by card text such as "for the turn," or "until the end of your opponent's turn, the effect persists even if the Digimon card that activated the effect becomes a digivolution card.
Q65
Does a Digimon's [On Play] effect activate when it's played in the breeding area?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A65
No, you can't activate effects on Digimon in your breeding area.
Basic Rules Attacking
Q66
What is an attack?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A66
An attack is a rule that allows a Digimon in the battle area to perform an offensive move (an "attack").
During your turn, suspend one of your Digimon that you want to have attack and make an attack declaration. At the same time as the declaration, the target of attack is chosen.
Either the opponent player or 1 of your opponent's suspended Digimon can be chosen as the attack target.
Upon an attack declaration, the timings will occur in the following order:
●① Attack declaration
▼② Counter timing
▼③ Block timing
▼④ Confirming if the attack is successful
▼⑤ End of attack
Once the end of attack timing ends, the attack will end.
The next timing won't begin until all of the processing is resolved for the current timing.
In addition, an attack declaration can't be made using a card during the turn it was played.
Q67
If a Digimon hatches in the breeding area and is moved to the battle area by an effect during the same turn, can that Digimon attack in the same turn?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A67
Yes, it can attack.
However, that Digimon can't attack if it was placed on the field by playing it.
Q68
If a Digimon is played into the breeding area, then it's moved to the battle area by an effect during the same turn, can that Digimon attack in the same turn?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A68
No, it can't attack.
A card can't attack during the same turn it was played.
Q69
If a Digimon digivolves from a card that was played this turn, can it attack in the same turn?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A69
No, it can't attack.
Q70
What happens if a Digimon that attacked is removed from the battle area by an effect?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A70
If a Digimon that attacked is no longer present, the attack will no longer succeed upon the "confirming if the attack is successful" timing.
However, transitions will still occur to all timings such as the counter timing and block timing.
Q71
What happens if an attack target Digimon is removed from the battle area by an effect?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A71
If an attack target Digimon is no longer present, the attack will no longer succeed upon the "confirming if the attack is successful" timing.
However, transitions will still occur to all timings such as the counter timing and block timing.
Q72
An attack target Digimon is removed from the battle area by an effect, so the attack target is no longer present.
Can a block by <Blocker> or a "change the attack target" effect then change that attack target?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A72
Yes, it can.
Q73
When a Digimon attacks, an "end the attack" effect activates, and the end of attack timing occured. Can the attacked player then activate a [Counter] effect?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A73
No, they can't.
If an effect that ends the attack activates, the end of attack timing comes immediately. A transition to the counter timing won't occur, therefore a [Counter] effect can't be activated.
Q74
Can an effect be used to attack while a Digimon is attacking?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A74
No, it can't.
Basic Rules Token Cards (Tokens)
Q75
What are token cards?Nov. 21, 2025 Updated
A75
Token cards are non-game cards used by effects. You may prepare as many token cards as you like, but you must prepare token cards if your deck or Digi-Egg deck includes a card with an effect that uses tokens.
Q76
Can I include token cards in a deck or Digi-Egg deck?Nov. 21, 2025 Updated
A76
No, you can't.
Token cards can't be included in a deck or Digi-Egg deck.
Q77
What kind of cards can I use when preparing token cards?Nov. 20, 2026 Updated
A77
You may use official token cards that are sold or distributed for the Digimon Card Game, or printed copies of images available for download.
Tokens used must include the information printed on that token's card.
Q78
If using a token card, do I have to declare the effect to be used with the token in advance to my opponent?Nov. 21, 2025 Updated
A78
No.
When using token cards, you don't have to declare the effect to be used with the token in advance.
Q79
When using a token card, does the card information have to be written on the token card?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A79
Yes, the card information must be written on the token cards to be used.
However, you don't have to declare the content or effects that will be used with token cards in advance.
Basic Rules Other Information
Q80
How do effects that use text like "play" or "use" differ from effects that use text like "you may play" or "you may use"?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A80
With effects that use text like "play" or "use," you must perform the action shown in the text whenever possible. For example, a "trash the top 3 cards of your deck" effect would mean that you must trash the top 3 cards of your deck. If you only have 2 or fewer cards in your deck, you must trash as many cards as possible until you have 0 cards in your deck.
Effects that use text like "you may" allow the player to choose whether they will activate that effect or not. For example, a "you may trash the top 3 cards of your deck" effect would mean that you may choose whether you will trash the cards from your deck or not. However, if you do choose to trash them, you must trash 3 cards whenever possible. You can't just trash 1 or 2 cards.
Q81
Does an [On Deletion] effect activate before or after the Digimon is placed in the trash?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A81
It activates after the Digimon is placed in the trash.
As soon as a Digimon is placed in the trash, its digivolution cards are removed, but when [On Deletion] or "when this Digimon is deleted" effects activate, the Digimon is treated as if it is still in the same state upon the timing when it was deleted from the battle area.
Q82
Will an "all of your Digimon [...] for the turn" effect affect Digimon that were played or were moved from the breeding area after the effect activates?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A82
Yes, effects that target "all of your (or your opponent's) Digimon" will also affect Digimon that were newly played or were moved from the breeding area while the effect is valid.
Q83
If a new Digimon is played while both an effect that adds to all Digimon's DP and an effect that reduces all Digimon's DP have activated, which effect is applied first?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A83
If effects that add and reduce are applied at the same time, the final modifying value is first calculated, and then the effects are applied to the targets.
For example, if an "all Digimon get -2000 DP" effect and an "all Digimon get +3000 DP" effect have activated, newly played Digimon will get +1000 DP as calculated from +3000 and -2000.
Q84
What happens to Digimon without DP in the battle area?Jun. 21, 2024 Updated
A84
Digimon without DP (Digimon without DP shown on their cards) can't be in the battle area according to the rules, therefore upon the rule check timing, the card is considered a card that doesn't belong in the battle area and is trashed.
The card doesn't belong in the battle area, but the Digimon isn't considered to be removed from the battle area, therefore effects such as "when this Digimon would leave the battle area" don't trigger.
Q85
If an effect reduces the DP of an opponent's Digimon to 0 and it's deleted, is it considered to be deleted by an effect?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A85
No. When a Digimon is deleted after its DP becomes 0, it's considered deleted by the game rules, not an effect.
Q86
If a Digimon with an [On Play] is played when an effect that reduces the DP of all Digimon has activated and that effect reduces the Digimon's DP to 0, does the [On Play] effect activate before it's deleted?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A86
No, it's deleted before the effect activates. However, "when a Digimon is played" conditions are fulfilled for effects on other Digimon or Tamers.
Q87
How do effects that allow choosing multiple targets and use text like "up to" differ from effects that don't use such text?Mar. 28, 2024 Updated
A87
Effects that use "up to" allow you to choose from 1 to the specified number when choosing targets.

Even before the [On Deletion] effect activates, <Overflow> will be processed and your memory will be reduced as soon as the Digimon ACE card is deleted and sent to the trash.

<Overflow>'s "from the battle area or under a card" has been rewritten to "from the area or under a card."
Cards with the previous text are treated the same as the cards with the rewritten text.

Basic Rules Security Check
Q539
What is a security check?Nov. 21, 2025 Updated
A539
A security check is a rule that allows a player to decrease their opponent's security stack. A security check is performed when a Digimon's attack is successful on a player that has 1 or more security cards.
The processing for a security check is performed using the following procedure.
① The player reveals their top security card. At such times, the checked card is removed from the security stack and is treated as not being in any particular area. If the checked card is a Digimon card, that card becomes a Security Digimon.
② Effects triggered upon a security check are processed. For example, [Security] effects, "when a card is removed from a security stack" effects, and "when a security check occurs" effects will trigger, then these effects are resolved.
③ If the case of a Security Digimon, a battle will occur between the Security Digimon and the Digimon that performed the security check.
④ A card revealed from a security check is trashed unless it belongs to an area. If an effect adds the card to the hand or places it in the battle area, it isn't trashed.
⑤ If the Digimon that performed the security check can perform another security check due to an effect such as <Security A. +1>, that Digimon then performs the additional check.

1 Digimon can have a maximum of 1 link card.

Basic Rules Battles
Q5776
What are battles?Feb. 6, 2026 Updated
A5776
During a battle, players compare the DP of the two battling cards.
After comparing DP, the card with the higher value is the winner, and the card with the lower value is the loser. If both cards have the same DP, both lose the battle and are deleted simultaneously.
After all of the processing is complete for the rules and effects at such times, the end of battle timing occurs.
Once all of the processing has been resolved for the end of the battle timing, the battle will end.
Q5777
If a Security Digimon loses a battle, is it then deleted?Feb. 6, 2026 Updated
A5777
No, it won't be deleted.
Only normal Digimon are deleted even when they lose a battle. Security Digimon aren't deleted. However, after the battle ends, the Security Digimon is trashed.

Basic Rules Assembly
Q5966
What does Assembly do?Mar. 13, 2026 Updated
A5966
Assembly refers to a rule where a player plays a Digimon card with Assembly requirements, places the specified number of the cards specified in that card's Assembly requirements from their trash under the Digimon card to be played, and the play cost is reduced by the value specified in that card's Assembly requirements.
Q5967
When can an Assembly be declared?
And do I have to perform an Assembly?Mar. 13, 2026 Updated
A5967
An Assembly can be declared and processed immediately before paying the play cost. If there are any "when [...] would be played" effects, the Assembly declaration comes after such effects.
In addition, an Assembly isn't mandatory. However, you can't declare an Assembly and then choose to place 0 cards.
Q5968
Can I declare an Assembly even when a card would be played by an effect?
And can I declare an Assembly when a card would be played by an effect from an area other than the hand?Mar. 13, 2026 Updated
A5968
Yes, you can declare an Assembly even when a card would be played by an effect.
Additionally, you can also declare an Assembly when a card would be played by an effect from an area other than the hand.
Q5969
Can I declare an Assembly even when an effect prevents the play cost from being reduced?Mar. 13, 2026 Updated
A5969
Yes, you can.
However, even though you can place the cards specified for the Assembly, the play cost for the Assembly can't be reduced, and you must pay the cost without the reduction. In addition, if you can't pay the original play cost without the reduction, you can't declare to play that card in the first place.
Q5970
How many of each card can I choose to place for an Assembly?Mar. 13, 2026 Updated
A5970
You must choose 1 of each specified card.
For example, if the Assembly requirements read "Assembly -6: [Titamon]×[SkullBaluchimon]," you must place 1 of each of the specified cards, and the play cost will be reduced by 6 if you place them.
However, when Assembly requirements such as "Assembly -3: 4 [Eyesmon: Scatter Mode] cards" specify the number of cards, you must choose that number of cards.
Q5971
Can I choose just some of the specified cards to be placed for an Assembly?Mar. 13, 2026 Updated
A5971
No, you can't choose just some of the cards to be placed.
For example, if the Assembly requirements read "Assembly -6: [Titamon]×[SkullBaluchimon]," the play cost will be reduced by 6 if you choose and place 1 [Titamon] and 1 [SkullBaluchimon]. When Assembly requirements such as "Assembly -3: 4 [Eyesmon: Scatter Mode] cards" specify the number of cards, you must choose 4 [Eyesmon: Scatter Mode] cards.
Q5972
If a card is played by an Assembly, is it considered to be played by an effect?Mar. 13, 2026 Updated
A5972
No, it isn't considered to be played by an effect.
Assembly is a rule that is processed when a card would be played.
However, if a card is played by a "play X" effect, it is considered to be played by an effect.
Q5973
If a card is played by an Assembly, will a "when played" inherited effect trigger?Mar. 13, 2026 Updated
A5973
Yes, it triggers.
Cards stacked underneath will be played in a state where they are considered to have their inherited effects.
Basic Rules Using an Option Card
Q6122
What does "use" mean, exactly?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6122
Using a card refers to a rule where an Option card's [Main] effect is activated. In order to use a card, its color requirements must be met.
① Declare that you are using a card, then reveal the Option card from your hand to your opponent.
② Pay the cost for the revealed card.
③ Once paid, the card's [Main] effect will activate immediately. After it activates, the Option card is trashed.
Q6123
Can I use multiple cards at the same time?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6123
No, you can't.
Q6124
If I use an Option card, when do "when [...] would be used" effects trigger?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6124
They will trigger immediately after the card to use is revealed.
However, the activation requirements have to be met.
Q6125
If I use an Option card, when can I activate "when you use" effects?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6125
You can activate them after the used card's [Main] effect has resolved and the Option card is trashed.
If an Option card is used by an effect, you can activate "when you use" effects after that effect has completely resolved.
Q6126
In what area is a used Option card considered to be placed before its [Main] effect is resolved?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6126
It isn't considered to be placed in any area until its [Main] effect is resolved.
For example, if you use an Option card from your hand, it won't be considered to be placed in any area, nor can it be referenced as a card in your hand.
Q6127
If a used Option card's [Main] effect places that card into the battle area, is the card then trashed?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6127
No, it isn't trashed if it's placed into the battle area.
Q6128
If an effect activates an Option card's [Main] effect, is that considered using an Option card?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6128
No, it isn't.
If an Option card's [Main] effect is directly activated by a method other than using it, it won't be considered use.
For example, if an Option card's [Main] effect is directly activated by a [Security] effect, it won't be considered use.
Q6129
Can I use an Option card and activate its [Main] <Delay> effect?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6129
No, you can't.
A [Main] <Delay> effect can be activated during your main phase after trashing that card in the battle area when there are no rules or effects that need to be processed.
Q6130
Can I use an Option card and use its [Main] effect to not process a "you may" effect?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6130
Yes, you can choose to not process it.
Q6131
Can I use an Option card and use its [Main] effect to not process a "by doing XX" effect?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6131
Yes, you can choose to not process it.
Basic Rules Arts Digivolve
Q6132
What is "Arts Digivolve," exactly?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6132
Arts Digivolve is a rule on DUAL cards.
Normally, an Option card is trashed after using it and activating its [Main] effect, but if a card has Arts Digivolve, the Arts Digivolve rule allows you to digivolve one of your cards on the field into that card without paying the cost instead of trashing it after use.
Q6133
When do I declare an Arts Digivolve?
And do I have to perform an Arts Digivolve?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6133
After a used Option card's [Main] effect has resolved, you can declare Arts Digivolve and process it when the card would be trashed. When an Arts Digivolve is performed, the processing will be resolved after the digivolution bonus draw is performed.
In addition, an Arts Digivolve isn't mandatory. However, you can't declare an Arts Digivolve and then choose to not digivolve.
Q6134
Can I declare an Arts Digivolve even when a card is used by an effect?
And can I declare an Arts Digivolve when a card is used by an effect from an area other than the hand?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6134
Yes, you can declare an Arts Digivolve even when a card is used by an effect. Additionally, you can declare an Arts Digivolve when a card is used by an effect from an area other than the hand.
Q6135
Can declare an Arts Digivolve even when the [Main] effect on a DUAL card's Option side is activated using a method other than "use an Option card"?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6135
No, you can't.
You can only declare an Arts Digivolve instead of trashing it after using an Option card.
Q6136
Does Arts Digivolve digivolve a Digimon by an effect?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6136
No. Arts Digivolve is a rule, so it doesn't digivolve a Digimon by an effect.
Q6137
Do I perform a digivolution bonus draw for digivolution using Arts Digivolve?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6137
Yes, you perform a digivolution bonus draw.
A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
Q6138
When using Arts Digivolve to digivolve, do [When Digivolving] effects and "when X digivolves" effects trigger?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6138
Yes, it triggers.
Q6139
Can I use Arts Digivolve to digivolve Digimon in my breeding area?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6139
Yes, you can.
Q6140
Can I use Arts Digivolve to digivolve without meeting the DNA digivolution requirements?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6140
No, you can’t ignore the digivolution requirements.
Q6141
Do "when X would digivolve" effects trigger for digivolution by Arts Digivolve?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A6141
Yes, they trigger.
