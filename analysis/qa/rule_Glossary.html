Glossary Game Field Names
Q451
AreaSep. 13, 2024 Updated
A451
This is the main area of the game. It includes the battle area and breeding area.
Q452
Battle AreaSep. 13, 2024 Updated
A452
This is the part of the area where cards are placed.
Digimon cards placed in this area are treated as Digimon, and Tamer cards placed in this area are treated as Tamers.
When the DP of a Digimon in the battle area becomes 0, it's deleted upon the rule check timing, and when a Digimon without DP is in the battle area, it's trashed upon the rule check timing.
Q453
Breeding AreaSep. 13, 2024 Updated
A453
This is the part of the area where hatched Digimon are placed.
Effects don't trigger or activate on Digimon in this area, and they can't be referenced or affected by a card's effects unless the effect specifies the breeding area.
Only 1 Digimon can be placed in the breeding area at a time, and a Digimon with DP can be moved to the battle area.
Q454

A454

Q455
Digi-Egg Deck ZoneSep. 13, 2024 Updated
A455

Q456
Trash (Recycle Bin) Sep. 13, 2024 Updated
A456
The trash is the area where trashed cards are placed. Cards are placed face-up in the trash.
Q457
Memory Gauge (Memory)Sep. 13, 2024 Updated
A457
This gauge shows the players' memory. 0 on the memory gauge is the center, the numbers on the left represent your memory, and the numbers on the right represent your opponent's memory.
For every 1 cost paid, the marker is moved 1 to the right on the memory gauge.
When the memory is at 1 or more on your opponent's side and all rules and effects have been resolved or processed, the turn will end.
Note that if the memory moves to 0 or more on your side at the end of the turn, the turn won't end.
Q458
The Security StackSep. 13, 2024 Updated
A458
A defensive wall that protects the player.
When an attack by your Digimon allows for 1 or more security checks, the attack is successful against your opponent, and your opponent has 0 security cards, you win the game.

Q519
At the end of a turn, I activated a "your Digimon attacks" effect and my Digimon attacked. At such times, does a transition to the counter timing occur before activation of other effects that trigger at the end of the turn?May. 8, 2026 Updated
A519
No, it doesn't.
For each of the timings whose transitions occur as a result of an attack, the processing must all be resolved during those timings.
Therefore, once all other effects that triggered upon the attack declaration have resolved, then a transition to the counter timing will occur.

Q533
<Overflow>Apr. 19, 2024 Updated
A533
<Overflow> is a rule that states that when a Digimon ACE card is sent from the area or from under a card in the area to another area, your memory must be reduced according to the specified value.
This rule is processed when a Digimon ACE card is a Digimon, digivolution card, or a card under a Tamer, and it is sent to an area other than the area.
<Overflow> is a rule, not an effect. In addition, you must immediately reduce your memory as specified as soon as the card is sent, even if a rule or effect is still being resolved.

What does (Rule) on a card mean?Aug. 1, 2024 Updated
A594
(Rule) is a specific rule on a card.

Q595
Does (Rule) always apply even when the card isn't in the battle area?Aug. 1, 2024 Updated
A595
Yes, it always applies, even when the card is in the breeding area, deck, or any other area besides the battle area.
For example, BT15-035 [Geremon]'s (Rule) also treats it as also having the name [Numemon], so it can digivolve into BT15-057 [Numemon (X Antibody)] from the breeding area, and a BT15-035 [Geremon] that was revealed from the deck can be added to the hand using RB1-017 [Numemon]'s effect.

Q596
What does "(Rule) Name: Also treated as [XX]" mean, exactly?Aug. 1, 2024 Updated
A596
It means that card's specific rule also treats it as originally having the name [XX].
For example, BT14-060 [Hi-Commandramon] is always treated as if it originally has both the [Hi-Commandramon] and [Commandramon] names.
This (Rule) allows this card to be chosen for text that specifies cards with the name [Commandramon].

A597

This (Rule) allows this card to be chosen for text that specifies cards with [Leomon] in their names.
However, this card's name is [Panjyamon], not [Leomon], therefore it can't be chosen for text that specifies cards with the name [Leomon].

It means that card's specific rule also treats it as originally having the card number [XX].

This (Rule) allows up to 4 total copies of this card and/or P-058 [Gammamon] in a deck.

Glossary face-down digivolution cards
Q4679
What are face-down digivolution cards?Jun. 13, 2025 Updated
A4679
They are digivolution cards that are placed face down.
As with normal digivolution cards, each card is counted as 1 digivolution card, but face-down digivolution cards are considered to have no card information.
In addition, their card information (color, card name, effects, etc.) can't be referenced, and their inherited effects can't trigger or activate.
Q4680
Can I change the stacking order of face-down digivolution cards?Jun. 13, 2025 Updated
A4680
No, you can't.
Q4681
Can I check the information on the front side of a face-down digivolution card?Jun. 13, 2025 Updated
A4681
Only the player of that card can check its information.
The opponent player can't check it.
Q4682
What happens when a face-down digivolution card is trashed?Jun. 13, 2025 Updated
A4682
It's placed face-up in the trash.
Q4683

A4683
No, it doesn't get additional DP.
Face-down digivolution cards are considered to have no card information, therefore their effects can't activate.

Q4994
If an effect moves face-down digivolution cards to a private area such as the deck, security stack, or hand, in what order are the cards placed?Nov. 20, 2026 Updated
A4994
The player who activated the effect chooses the cards' order and places them. Only the owner of the cards can look at what they are.
If the player who activated the effect is moving their own Digimon's face-down digivolution cards, they can look at the information on those cards when doing so.
If you are moving your opponent's Digimon's digivolution cards, you choose their order and place them without looking at the information on the cards, while your opponent is able to look at them.
Glossary Orientation
Q7483
What does orientation mean?Sep. 18, 2026 Updated
A7483
Orientation refers to a card being unsuspended or suspended.
When in the portrait orientation, the card is unsuspended. In the landscape, it is suspended.
Also, when a card's orientation changes, an unsuspended card suspends and a suspended one unsuspends.
Q7484
If there's an effect that prevents a card from suspending or unsuspending, can its orientation be changed to that orientation?Sep. 18, 2026 Updated
A7484
No, it cannot.
